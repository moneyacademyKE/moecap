About Moe Capital

Independent financial research, published in the open. Based in Nairobi, Kenya.

Moe Capital publishes equity research and runs the NSE ROIC Terminal, a live dashboard covering 69 companies listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange. We also publish US equity ideas, book notes and a weekly idea feed.

How the numbers are made

Primary documents, not summaries. Kenyan financials are read from the filings themselves — audited annual reports, half-year statements and issuer announcements from the NSE and CMA media libraries — then computed into ROIC and efficiency metrics. Where a filing is unreadable or the period is ambiguous, the company stays out of the dataset rather than being filled in with a guess.

Prices come from the exchange feed and refresh hourly. Every figure in the terminal traces back to a document, and the research pipeline that maintains it is versioned, so an error can be traced and corrected rather than quietly overwritten.

What we are not

  • Not a hot-take account. Nothing here is a trade alert.
  • Not paid placement. No issuer pays for coverage, and the exchange library carries no affiliate links.
  • Not a promise. Research is analysis, not a forecast, and not personal advice — see Terms.
