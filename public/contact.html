Before you write

  • Found a data error? Send the company, the metric and the filing page. Corrections are welcome and get published, not buried.
  • Want to work together? Say what you are trying to do and the capital involved. The rate card covers scope and fees.
  • Asking for a tip? We don't give personalised recommendations outside an engagement. The research is public — read it and decide.
