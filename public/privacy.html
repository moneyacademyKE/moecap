Privacy

Short version: this site does not identify you, and we don't sell anything about you.

What this site stores

No accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter capture on moecapital.com. Two things are kept in your browser's local storage, and neither ever leaves your device:

  • which companies you have starred in the watchlist strip;
  • whether the NSE terminal is in simple or expert mode.

There are no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics scripts and no cookies set by this site.

What our servers see

Pages and price data are served through Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested path) for security and abuse prevention. Price and holdings updates are fetched from our own endpoint; those requests carry no personal data.

When you contact us

Messages you send over WhatsApp, Telegram, X or email are used to reply to you and to keep a record of any engagement. They are not added to a marketing list. Ask us to delete your messages and we will, unless we are required to keep them for a live engagement or by law.

Outbound links

The site links out to Substack, Google Drive, YouTube and other sites. Once you leave moecapital.com, their privacy terms apply, not ours.

Changes

If this policy changes, the change will be visible on this page. Questions about privacy: contact us.
